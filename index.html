Belinda Li

Hi! I am an incoming PhD student to MIT, where I will be working with Jacob Andreas at the language & intelligence lab @ MIT.

Previously, I spent an amazing year at Facebook AI Applied Research, on the AI Integrity team, where I worked on applying state-of-the-art techniques from AI and NLP research to combat integrity problems faced by Facebook. I also did some (more pure) research in natural language processing. Before that, I obtained my B.S. in Computer Science at the University of Washington, where I was fortunate to work with Luke Zettlemoyer. You can view more in my CV here.

If you are an undergrad interested in NLP or machine learning research, please feel free to reach out to me! You can view my contact policy here.
