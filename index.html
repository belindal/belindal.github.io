Hi! I am a software engineer at Facebook AI Applied Research, where I work on applying state-of-the-art techniques from AI and NLP research to combat integrity problems faced by Facebook. I also do (more pure) research in natural language processing.

Previously, I obtained my B.S. in Computer Science at the University of Washington, where I was privileged to work with Professor Luke Zettlemoyer.
